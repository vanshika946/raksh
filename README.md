# rakshnova
RakshNova: AI Disaster Intelligence & Emergency Response

A hackathon prototype dashboard for disaster risk analysis, early warnings, high-risk zone mapping and emergency response planning. It is built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no build step and no dependencies.

Prototype only. Region names, populations, shelters and the risk model are fictional or simulated. This is not official government or emergency data. In a real emergency, call your local emergency number (112 in India).

Features
Live Data Feeds: real weather from Open-Meteo and real earthquakes from USGS, refreshed automatically.
Disaster Overview: per-hazard cards (flood, earthquake, fire, extreme weather).
Risk Analysis Engine: a transparent, rule-based score from scenario inputs, with contributing factors.
Early Warning Alerts: generated from zone risk and from your own analyses.
High-Risk Zone Map: a stylised map of a fictional region with selectable zones.
Emergency Response Panel: evacuation guidance, safe zones, contacts and a checklist.
AI Situation Briefing: a rule-based narrative for the selected scenario, zone or alert.
