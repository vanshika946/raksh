    How the simulated score works

    Risk % = 40% location hazard exposure for the chosen disaster + 35% severity + 25% population density, plus an amplifier when exposure and severity are both very high. Levels: Low < 30, Moderate 30–54, High 55–77, Critical 78+.

    Live inputs. When the feed for the chosen region is LIVE, exposure blends the region's static susceptibility with a live 0–1 signal. Flood, fire and weather: 60% susceptibility + 40% live signal. Earthquake: susceptibility plus 70% of its remaining headroom × the live signal (quiet periods never lower it, because earthquakes cannot be predicted).

    Flood signal = rain over the past 24 h, forecast 24 h and the rate now. Fire = heat, dryness and gusts, damped by recent rain. Weather = gusts, rain rate, peak hourly rain and thunderstorm codes. Earthquake = magnitude and distance of USGS events within 300 km in the last 24 h. A small random variation is added only in SIMULATED mode.
